Inscene report
This week-end happened the first INSCENE, the only belgian party since the regretted Wired. Though there was less people than the organizers expected (about 50 people), the fun was there.<BR>New records have been established in 4kg HD throwing competition : the shortest distance (50 cm) and the longest (10m30) ;-)<BR>Due to the lack of competitors, there were not many entries in the competitions ; but we discovered great prods, like the 100kb game from PPPTeam and a great linux demo from Aspirine - the only entry in the demo compo :(<BR>To conclude, this was a very friendly party which was worth it . I hope that there will be more cool guys next year and that INSCENE will become the new Wired - for the fun, it's on the right way ;) - You can download the prods on scene.org.
