TP8 Life report
Life report from The Party 1998 by Sparcus of Nostalgia
2:42PM Monday, December 28

Ok, you might think that it's a little bit late to start this party report,
but this morning I spoke to Dr.Yes on the irc, and he asked me to write a life report for Orange Juice, so that explains the delay :-)
I'm btw very happy that I'm still able to write this report, because we had a very scary moment during our bustrip. There was a storm going over Denmark, and when we were driving across a high bridge the wind grabbed the bus, and we almost hit the edge of the bridge. Fortunately we had a good driver, but he couldn't avoid that the emergency escape hatch was blown away from the
roof. Due to this fact we had to drive the last few hours with a big hole in our roof, which isn't the most comfortable way of driving through Denmark during wintertime, that I can assure you :-)

Anyhow, we finaly made it to the partyplace, and after paying our entrance fee (which like usual costed a lot of money and a lot of time), we started unloading the bus. The Quad guys had some reservation problems with their seats, but after about an hour the dutch guys had installed their stuff all over the place.

There's not much to tell about this year's party to people who have already been here during one of the previous editions. The organizers still don't care about quality. They still have the same lousy logo, the booklets are exactly the same like last year, the bigscreen almost looks the same. I can understand that people will come to the Party anyhow, but personally I'd like to seem more innovation once in a while instead of just reusing all the old stuff and designs.


The netwerk also sucks like usual. In hall 3 you can only get online if you have a 100mbit network card. Fortunately I was able to buy the last one :-)
Since there are still loads of people with "meager" 10mbit coax cards I think organizers are responsible for taking care of these people. Connection to the party ftp server was a major pain in the ass, and people got thrown out of the irc constantly. I heard a rumour that at first there weren't enough people in the network crew with the knowledge to run such a big network, so maybe that explains the problems.

Furthermore there were some other minor problems, like the bigscreen in hall 3 that didn't work for a few hours, so it was very hard to keep track of the competition starting times. In general I think the announcement of the compo times is something the organizers should take more care off since the announcements (which announcements? ;-) are really bad. And ofcourse a lot
of names were written down incorrectly because a lot of the organizing crew doesn't know shit about demos (which explains the greetings to Tpam in the thrown off again, another restart at 7
Fudge. The Fugde intro looked like a combination of effects from a lot of their previous productons, and it really kicked ass. The intro by Halcyon was also very original.

I missed about half of the amiga intro compo due to the bad announcements, but I don't think I missed much. Although the entries were all of a reasonably quality, I don't think the amiga scene is as good and advanced anymore as it used to be. Everything I saw in the amiga intros has already been done on pc, but then better.

The java compo had only 2 entries. The one by Digital Nerds was very good (especially the blobby effect that comes after the "don't try this at home" message). I can't remember the name of the people that made the other one, but it was a reasonable demo, although the design wasn't very good. The organizers fucked up the order of the entries (how the hell can you mix only two entries????) so the voting for the java compo will probably be
incorrect. The Digital Nerds definately have to win. The only thing I was missing is a Complex demo, because I think it's about time that Digital Nerds beat Complex in the java competition :-)
Spiff of Yodel is standing right next to me at this moment, and telling me that Snooky and Voodoo of Yodel also made a java demo which wasn't shown. So one can say that the organizers truely fucked up the java compo this year!!! :-(

The last compo I've seen when writing this first part of the report is the C64 graphics compo. There weren't much entries, but some of them were extremely good (unfortunately I can't remember any of the names). Ok, that's about it for this moment, since you already know the rest. Half
the scene is here, there is a good party atmosphere, eventhough the place is loaded with game lamers, people are plugging their demos in fullcolor flyers and posters everywhere, Denmark is still as expensive as it always used to be, etc. Only one thing seems to be different this year, and that's the fact that I wasn't waken up by an organizer when I slept in one of the party halls :-)
