Note from Sparcus / Nostalgia : this was not the first separation between house and nonhouse music. House has always been very popular in the dutch scene. In the past at a lot of dutch parties house didn't stood a chance against nonhouse because it wasn't even played or ended up very low because of jury voting. House people started to complain about this more and more. The REW (who was the music editor for Imphobia for a while as you might remember) once wrote an article about it and proposed a separated house compo. This is the reason why at X'97 Takeover (where The REW was main organiser) the first separated house competition was held. This idea was immediately copied by the organizers of Bizarre'97. At Ambience'97 they even held more separated compos, like a hardcore compo for example. I guess you can say this is a typical dutch thing :-)
