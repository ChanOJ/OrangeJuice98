  For the ones who know State of The Art by Spaceball on Amiga, here is an ANSI adaptation on PC! With the UAE emulator, Vodka / Saturne manage to grep the graphic sequence in ansi! Add to it a home made ANSI-Reader by Z/Pulpe and you'll get something quite near of the original. The module is the original one by Travolta.
