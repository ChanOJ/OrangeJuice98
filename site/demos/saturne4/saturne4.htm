Saturne IV

[ >TOP / >PC DEMOS / AMIGA DEMOS / 64K / >GFX / >RAY / >MODULES ]

Usually the Saturne Party takes place in april, but this year, two Saturne party took place: the Saturne 3 in april, and the 4th edition the 1st, 2nd and 3rd november 1996, both, as usually in Chelles near Paris.

This Saturne Party 4 welcomed 502 persons (600 in April ) in the cultural center of Chelles in Seine et Marne. No marking points except maybe for the Friday morning power shut down, and the delays in the megademo competition.
During the party, it was impossible to get the productions. Lets hope it's not due to the cd the organizers are making to regroup all of them... Troubles allso with the multi-channel compo in which many prods were refused, making quite some unhappy composers.
