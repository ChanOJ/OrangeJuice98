Wired 96

[ >TOP / >DEMOS / >INTROS 64K / >4K INTROS / >GFX / >RAY / >3D ANIMATIONS / >MODULES ]

The Wired is organized by Imphobia (known for its e-mag) and Antares in Wasmes nearby Mons in Belgique. It begins on Friday 1st november 1996 at 11h00 and end on Sunday at 17h00.

Marking point was the departure of the french Oxygene group (there preparing a game) and the lack of inovating megademo. On the other hand intros confirmed the reputation of quality of the Wired.

The demo were presented on a Pentium 120 with 8Mo of RAM and a Tseng Labs ET6000 video card, for the sound the choice was given between a Gravis Ultrasound Pnp (1 Mo) or a Sound Blaster AWE32.

For more information, the official invitation is available here.
