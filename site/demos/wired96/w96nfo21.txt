 * GENERAL RULES FOR ALL COMPETITIONS

  - The organizers  reserve  the right  to make changes  to  the competitions
    rules and to disqualify any entry which is not suitable to take part into
    the competition for any reasons.

  - Competitions are held for PC platforms.

  - Every group is free to contribute with as many Demos, Raytrace, 64kb 
    intro as they want to.
    As for the solo competition (gfx, music, 4kb intro), only one entry per
    graphic artist/musician/coder will be accepted in each competition.

  - The competitors MUST be present at the party place in order to compete.
    For group productions, at least one member of the team has to be present.

  - All entries must be delivred in time for the deadline at the information 
    desk.  This is very important if we want to have enough time  to process 
    every contribution.

  - All contribution disks must be marked with the following information: 
     * Which Competition, 
     * Competitor Name/Group 
     * Boot Config (when needed).
    
  - Contribution disks won't be returned. 

  - The organiser have the rights to remove a competition if there isn't 
    enough competitors to make a competition out of it.
  
| - For demos & intros, only 100% VGA Register level compatible graphics mode 
|   or VESA 2.0.

  - If your demo still requires any interaction, we want ONE people of the
    competing in the Control Room.

  - All entries  for the PC intros (4k and 64k),  Gfx and  Music competitions
    will be spread by the organisers after the party.

  - For the PC demo competition entries,  it's up to you to tell us if we can
    release or not your product at the party place. You will have *ONE* month
    after the Party to finish  and release your product,  else we will spread
    the party version. (we won't make any exeptions).

 * PC DEMO COMPETITION

  - We will run the demo on a Pentium 120 Mhz

  - Video card: Tseng Labs ET6000

    *WARNING* !  Our video projector is only able to handle resolutions up to
    640x480 !  Using a too high resolution won't damage it but will produce a
    garbled screen.

  - NO TSENG LABS SPECIFIC HARDWARE FUNCTIONS!
  - We will use MSDOS 6.3.
  - Available Memory configuration :

		- HIMEM Alone
		- HIMEM+EMM386
		- QEMM
		- Clean Boot

    You will have the choice between one of those configs, or bring your 
    own boot disk.

  - Sound Cards available: - Gravis UltraSound Plug&Play 1Mb
			   - Creative Labs Sound Blaster Awe32

  - The demo must be able to run from HardDisk.
  - Pure animation files are not accepted.
  - Maximum base memory requirement is 600Kb 
  - Maximim memory requirement: 8 Megs.
  - File Size limit is 6144 Kb.

  - We know by  our experience of demo coders  that the competition demos are
    often not bugfixed for all hardwares and sometimes run through batchfile.
    Therefore, you will be allowed to test your demos TWO TIMES on the compe-
    tition computer before we show them to the audience.   But  please  don't 
    come just before the deadline...

 * PC 64KB INTRO COMPETITION

  - The intros must run on the same machine than the demos.
  - Same video modes than for demos.
  - File Size limit is 64Kb.
  - Maximum running time is 8 minutes.
  - Maximum base memory requirement is 600Ko
  - Pure animations files are not accepted

 * PC 4KB INTRO COMPETITION

  - The 4Kb intros must run on the same machine than the demos.
  - File Size limit is 4096 Bytes.
  - Maximum running time is 3 minutes.
  - Only 100% VGA Register level compatible graphic modes and VESA 1.2
  - Maximum base memory requirement is 600Kb
  - No sound ! (This is a coding competition)
  - Animations files not accepted ;)

 * MULTICHANNEL MUSIC COMPETITION

  - Maximum size: 1024 Kb
  - Maximum amount of channels: 32
  - ANY digital tracked format allowed (no MIDI, adlib, SID; nor WAV, VOC,...)
  - Soundcard: Gravis Ultrasound PnP 1Mb (Sound Blaster AWE 32 on request)
| - Players: MOD, S3M -> Latest Capamod Player by Capacala
|                  XM -> FastTracker II by Triton
|                  IT -> Last Impulse Tracker
| - Any other format will be played by latest Cubic Player, if it supports it.
| - It's up to you to bring your own player if you don't like ours ;-)
  - Songs that exceed 3'30" will be fade out after that time.
  - We will not show the author's name while playing.
  - A preselection will be made if the number of entries exceed a reasonable
    number.

 * GFX COMPETITION

  - Max. 640x480 x 256 colors
  - Accepted Format: PCX, GIF, LBM, BMP & All formats supported by SEA (All in
    8bit, any 24bit file will be automaticly disqualified)
  - We will use SEA with UNIVBE 5.3 vesa driver loaded to display the pictures.
  - The name of the competitors must not be present on the picture.
    (The release version can of course have your signature).
  - You should include on the disk both versions: Signed & not Signed in two 
    different archives (with the compo version named Nosigned.zip).
  - The competitors must deliver 3 significative steps (compo version not
    included) of their picture to the organisers.
  - Pictures needs to be 'pixel hand-drawn', therefore raytrace, scans or
    anything else are *NOT* allowed.
  - Pictures can't have been used before in any productions or competitions.
    (Except in demos/intros first shown at the Wired 96).
  - Any picture can be disqualified by the organizers if it doesn't fit the
    rules. A preselection will be made if the number of entries exceed a rea-
    sonable number.
 
 * RAYTRACE GRAPHICS COMPETITION

  - Maximum resolutions: 640x480x16M
  - Allowed format: TGA, JPG, BMP, PCX, GIF, LBM (Pc format) & all formats
    supported by SEA.
  - All pictures are shown with SEA and UNIVBE 5.3 loaded.
  - You'll need to bring three significative steps (not Wire, Flat, Phong and
    Textured Phong/Raytrace versions of the same picture) of your picture,
    these pictures obviously don't need to be in high res or true-color mode.

 * RAYTRACE MOVIES COMPETITION

  - Maximum size: 100 Mb (Anim+Sound)
  - Maximum resolution: 320x200 x 16.7 million colors (TrueColor)
  - Maximum Replay Rate: 24 fps
  - You can use an Audio CD as Music Source.
  - Video tape recording (PAL only) and harddisk transfert allowed.
    ( NO TAPES! They are Too Slow To Restore )
  - It's up to you to give us the adequate Player.

 * SURPRISE COMPETITIONS

  As usual some surprise competitions will be held.  
  This year again, our weaked brains have thought about many great surprise
  compos. But that would be stupid to tell you about them now, wouldn't it?

 * ANSI COMPETITION

  - Maximum size: 80x200
      Ansi can be viewed either in 80x25 or 80x50 mode.
      Ansi will be viewed in ANSI and VGA mode.
      It will scroll at a speed of a 14.4K modem.
  - NO ANIMATION!
