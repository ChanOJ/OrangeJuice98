LTP'97

[ >TOP / >DEMOS / >GFX / >MODULES ]

First edition of a promising coding partie by Ribbon. It was a so called private party, fully reserved for the scene members (demo & art) and without any invitation (only in gossip knews). A quite usefull information: LTP means Lucky & Tigrou Party.

It welcomed 87 people in a room for 50 at Clermont de l'Oise. Were present:
PSYCKO - JINRIKISHA - KAOTIK BREIN - SILICON - AURORA BOREALIS - EDENA - DSK - REVELATION - SKYTECH - FLOWER CORP - ATROCITY - ZEN - OVERFLOW - PSY-S - DENTIFRICE - ENIGMA - CENTEK SARL - ADRENALINE - BENEDICTION - MORTEL - PULPE - ARKOS - SYNDROME - ECLIPSE - LIVE!PULSE - ORANGE - CYCLONE - IMPHOBIA - MELTING POT - N-ZONE - BLAF! - MWB - BOMB
And a television team of C: (sponsors).
