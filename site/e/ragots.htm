[07/08/98]
- Daskmig #10 will be released this autumn. It'll be released in three versions; one main CD-ROM version featuring a lot of good music by the band of Jisemdu/Dance called State of Mind, Twaddler/Proxima's band called RekeRagu. These will be replaced by modules in the ZIP-version to be spread via the internet, and there will also be a WWW-version with no music at all. The CD-ROM will also cover the scene from 1986 to 1998 where most Amiga and PC demos have been reviewed with screen-shots. There will also be an enormous scrolltext-index where we will put all scrolltexts we get hold of. Enough reading for anyone for at least a week or so!
