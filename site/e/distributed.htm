Distributed computing

Orange Juice have created a Distributed.net team, named The Demoscene Team (ID 7238). You can get info on it here.

What is Distributed.net ? As they say :

<< As a "loosely knit" group of computer users from all over the world, we take up challenges which require a lot of computing power. We solve these by distributing the cpu power needed over the computers of our members. That's why we are called "distributed.net". Read more about it in our mission statement. >>

IMPORTANT ! It works on a LOT of computers (Amiga, Linux, BSD, Windows, Macintosh, ...) and you do not need to be permanently connected to the net.

To join The Demoscene Team, just do this :
- get the client of the RC5 challenge
- install it on your computer
- set it with your email
- run it ;-)
- wait to be listed in the individual list (you'll automatically receive an email with a password)
- join the The Demoscene Team team (edit your properties a the bottom of your stats sheet)
