Demo description

to have your demo appear in OJ, send following informations to ojuice@???? :

- demo name
- group name
- coder(s)
- graphist(s)
- musician(s)
- DOS extender and/or OS
- sound system if not proprietary (eg: MIDAS)
- supported sound cards
- video system (VESA, DirectX, ...), video modes
- objective description
- 0 to 4 snapshots (cf. on the right)
- inet adress of the archive (preferably on hornet or arosnet, otherwise be sure of its perennity)

  





snapshots: included in 100x75 pixels, 4096 bytes maximum. (tips: progressive jpg, compression rate of 15 with PSP 4.12). each one could be associated wit a real size version (eg: 320x240) of 35 kB maximum (tips: progressive jpg, compression rate of 10 with PSP 4.12) wich will appear when clicking on the snapshot. attach them to the email (no uuencode).
